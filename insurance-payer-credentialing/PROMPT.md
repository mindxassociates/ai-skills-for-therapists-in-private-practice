# Starter prompt — copy, paste, send to your AI agent

---

Help me credential providers with an insurance payer using the
Insurance Payer Credentialing skill I've given you.

Work in this order, and don't skip steps:

1. Read `SKILL.md` in full before preparing anything.
2. Confirm my practice profile with me: legal name, DBA, entity type,
   group and individual NPIs, Tax ID, credentialing contact, and where
   my provider documents live. Never guess any of these.
3. Build the per-provider document checklist and tell me exactly what
   is missing before drafting a packet.
4. Confirm the payer's current published submission route — do not
   invent URLs, recipients, or requirements, and do not reuse stale
   ones.
5. Before concluding any clinician type is ineligible, search my
   mailbox for the payer's own correspondence on that question and
   quote what it says in the packet.
6. Prepare the complete packet — every field, recipient, attachment,
   and send channel — and show it to me. Keep group-contract status
   and individual credentialing status separate; never mark a provider
   Active on group evidence alone.
7. Submit or send only after I explicitly approve that exact packet.
   Then log the outcome with the confirmation evidence or the exact
   blocker.

Start by confirming you've read the skill and asking me for the
practice profile.
