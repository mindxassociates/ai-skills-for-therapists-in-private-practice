# Snoooz Inbox Automation Kit

Set up **Snoooz.ai email automation for a solo or small therapy practice**, adapted from a working group-practice system: directory inquiries answered behind hard safety gates, EAP referrals handled without invented availability, existing clients helped with logistics, billing questions routed instead of guessed at — and promotional, notification, and credentialing mail excluded before any of it can trigger a reply.

This kit is a playbook, not a copy of a working practice's account. Your agent builds the version for your practice from your own confirmed panels, rates, booking links, and contacts.

## What you get

- **Gated inquiry replies** for Psychology Today and other directories, one rule per language you serve. Every prospective-client reply carries the same seven gates: age, client type, insurance/panels, location/license state, crisis, session format, and fees/sliding-scale floor.
- **EAP handling that respects the calendar problem.** Referral-style EAP inquiries get a send-ready reply; availability-style EAP requests stay Draft forever, because Snoooz cannot see your calendar and must never invent a time.
- **Existing-client and billing routing.** Clients get logistics help (video links, rescheduling) and no clinical advice; billing and insurance questions route to the inbox or person you name, with answers drawn only from your confirmed panel list — never a copay or benefit estimate.
- **An exclusion system built first.** Promotional mail, newsletters, system notifications, EAP credentialing mail, and mail you sent yourself never get an auto-reply.
- **Labels and categories** created before the rules, so every rule files its mail consistently from day one.
- **Two optional rules, off by default:** a Google Voice text template and a vacation auto-reply — each with the failure modes documented before you decide.
- **A staged go-live:** simulations first, Draft mode while you review tone, Send only on your explicit go-ahead, and verification by reopening every saved rule.

## Who this fits

- A solo therapist whose directory inquiries arrive by email and eat the evening.
- A small practice (a handful of clinicians sharing one inquiry inbox) that wants group-grade gates without group-grade machinery — no per-provider forwarding, BCCs, or roster syncs.
- A practice listed on Psychology Today or other email-based directories, with or without EAP contracts.
- A bilingual practice that needs its inquiry replies to follow per-language naming and booking-link rules exactly.

## How to use this kit

Hand the **whole folder** to your AI agent and say:

> "Set up Snoooz for my practice using this kit. Start with the setup checklist — interview me through config.md before building anything."

Routes: native skill support (install the folder) / coding-workspace agent (point it at `SKILL.md`) / chat-only agent (upload all `.md` files, paste the prompt from `PROMPT.md`).

## Boundaries

- **Your facts, confirmed for this practice.** Panels, rates, and links from a group practice, agency, or platform you also work with do not carry over until you confirm them for this practice.
- **Draft is the default until you say Send.** Availability requests never leave Draft.
- **Snoooz has limits you accept explicitly.** No once-per-sender cap, no stop-after-a-human-reply setting, and no "AI chooses not to reply" switch — the prompt gates are the safety system, which is why the kit never treats them as optional.
- **Not a clinical tool.** Replies give no clinical advice; a crisis mention gets crisis resources first, in every rule that can receive one.
