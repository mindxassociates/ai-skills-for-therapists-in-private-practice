# Auto-Created Listing Takedown Kit

Directory companies scrape the public NPPES registry and auto-publish a profile — sometimes a whole mini-website — for nearly every clinician in the country. Nobody asks, nobody notifies you. The pages routinely include details the operator **assumed**: office hours you never set, "accepting new patients," specialties you don't have, a wrong city, even appointment buttons for a practice the operator doesn't run. They rank in Google for your name.

This kit is the playbook for getting them taken down, reporting the pattern, and keeping them down.

## What you get

- **A complete inventory method.** Search by NPI and legal name (pages are built from NPPES legal names), verify every page is really you — name *and* NPI — and discard same-name strangers safely.
- **Per-site removal playbooks.** Dr. Leonardo (millions of auto-created clinician sites with fabricated hours), Healthgrades, WebMD Care, and the NPI mirrors / data brokers (npiprofile, npi.usadb, datalabs.health, Topline) — each with its actual removal route, or an honest "no route exists."
- **The validation discipline.** Most of these flows end with "check your inbox" — the takedown isn't real until you open the validation link (usually from your spam folder). The kit covers the one-claim-per-email rule, per-clinician addresses for groups, and the token gotchas that make links fail.
- **Abuse reports that go somewhere.** Copy-ready filings for the FTC (ReportFraud.ftc.gov), Google Search Quality, the site's host/CDN, and its domain registrar — with the evidence each one actually needs.
- **A monthly sweep.** A recurring check that re-opens every known URL, re-files removals for pages that resurrect, scouts for new auto-created pages, and sends you one summary.

## Who this fits

- A solo clinician who found a weird profile with their name on it — or wants to check whether one exists.
- A group practice that wants every clinician's pages handled in one pass, with one admin inbox receiving all validation emails.
- Any practice whose Google results are polluted by directories it never joined.

Routes: native skill support (install the folder) / coding-workspace agent (point it at `SKILL.md`) / chat-only agent (upload all `.md` files, paste the prompt from `PROMPT.md`).

## Boundaries

- **Removal only.** Some sites make you "claim" a listing in order to delete it. Claim only to delist — never pay for upsells, never build out the profile, unless you deliberately choose claim-and-correct instead.
- **Some sites won't remove a practicing clinician.** Healthgrades' policy is the known example; the kit's fallback is an exception request plus correction of the worst errors, stated honestly as a fallback.
- **Some mirrors have no removal route at all.** The kit names them and tells you the only real lever (the source NPPES record) instead of pretending otherwise.
- **Reports are factual.** Every filing states what the page claims, what is fabricated, and when removal was requested — no exaggeration; the pattern is damning enough on the facts.
