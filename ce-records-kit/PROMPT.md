---

Set up and maintain my continuing-education records using the CE Records Kit in this workspace.

Follow the kit exactly:

1. **Requirements first.** For each license I hold, build the worksheet in `references/requirements-and-categories.md` from the board's **current published rules** (cite the page for every number): cycle dates, total hours, each sub-requirement, one-time mandates, method/delivery limits, and the reporting system of record. Show me the worksheets for confirmation before anything else.
2. **Build the tracker** per `references/tracker-and-certificates.md`, then populate it from my certificates — the certificate is the source of truth for title, provider, date, and hours (including ethics/supervision splits). Where a tracker I already keep disagrees with a certificate, flag it and fix the tracker.
3. **Reconcile** per `references/reporting-system-reconciliation.md`: get my official transcript from the reporting system, diff it against the tracker, and prepare the list of what's missing. Enter records only after I approve the list, then re-pull the transcript and verify the totals match, category by category.
4. **Report status the kit's way:** per license — hours banked vs. required for the total and **each sub-category separately**, cycle end date, and the specific deficit if any. Never report only a grand total.
5. Never check an attestation box, certify, or submit a renewal in my name. Prepare everything, show me, and stop at the signature line.
