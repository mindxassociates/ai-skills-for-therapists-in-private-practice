# Reconciliation — tracker vs. the official transcript

The board renews against its reporting system, not against your tracker. Reconciliation is the routine that keeps the two identical. Run it at least **once mid-cycle and once before renewal** — and any time the tracker and a transcript glance disagree.

## Systems vary; the routine doesn't

Some boards use CE Broker; some run their own portal; some are self-report with audit. The steps below say "reporting system" — map them to yours, and note the mapping (and how a transcript is obtained) on the worksheet.

## The routine

1. **Get the official transcript** for the current cycle: every recorded course, its hours and category credits, and the system's own totals/deficit display. Save a dated copy (PDF or screenshot) with the cycle's records — "the transcript as of <date>" is evidence; a login page is not.
2. **Normalize both sides.** Transcript titles rarely match certificates character-for-character (truncated, re-cased, provider-named). Match on date + provider + hours first, title second. Build the diff in three buckets:
   - **In tracker, not on transcript** → the entry list (candidates to enter).
   - **On transcript, not in tracker** → courses the licensee forgot to log (providers sometimes report directly). Add tracker rows from the transcript data, flagged for certificate-chasing — they count on the transcript, but the audit proof may still be missing.
   - **On both, but different** (hours or category credits differ) → the discrepancy list. The certificate arbitrates; the fix follows the system's correction process.
3. **Licensee approves the entry list** — the agent shows exactly what will be entered: course, provider, date, hours, category splits, per entry. Approval is per list, explicit.
4. **Enter only the approved, missing records.** Field values come from the certificates. If the system's entry form requires an attestation checkbox *as part of entry*, the licensee does that step themselves (or approves in the moment, per the practice's own rule) — the agent does not check it on a standing instruction.
5. **Re-pull the transcript and verify.** Totals per category on the transcript now equal the tracker's per-license summary, and the entry-list items appear. Anything that didn't land goes back to step 2 as a fresh discrepancy — "submitted" is not "recorded" until the transcript shows it.
6. **Update the tracker:** Reported = Yes with the verification date for everything now on the transcript; save the new dated transcript copy.

## Common traps

- **Provider-reported duplicates:** a big CE platform reports the course *and* the licensee enters it → the total inflates and the board's audit finds a mess. The diff step exists to catch this *before* entering.
- **Wrong-cycle entries:** a course entered under the current cycle that was completed last cycle. Date-check every entry-list item against the cycle window.
- **Category credit ≠ hours:** some systems record the course hours but not the ethics split unless the entry specifies it. Verify the *category* totals on the re-pull, not just the grand total.
- **Name mismatch:** the licensee's name in the system differs from the certificate (middle initial, former name). Note the exact system spelling on the worksheet; it's the match key.
