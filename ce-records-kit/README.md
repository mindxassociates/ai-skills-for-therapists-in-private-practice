# CE Records Kit

Keep your **continuing-education records renewal-ready, all cycle long** — not in a panic the month your license expires. This kit is a workflow your AI agent runs with you: pin down exactly what each of your licenses requires, keep one tracker fed by your actual certificates, reconcile it against your board's reporting system (CE Broker, or whatever your state uses) so only missing courses get entered, and watch the category totals — total hours, ethics, supervision, and one-time mandates — fill up with time to spare.

## What you get

- **A requirements worksheet per license** — board, renewal cycle dates, total hours, sub-requirements (ethics, supervision, cultural competence…), one-time trainings, delivery/method rules, and the reporting system of record — sourced from the board's own published rules, never from memory.
- **One tracker** with a row per course: title, provider, completion date, total hours, and the sub-category hours (ethics, supervision…) the certificate actually grants, plus subject areas and which licenses the course counts toward.
- **A certificates library** that stays the source of truth: when the tracker and a certificate disagree, the certificate wins and the tracker is corrected.
- **A reconciliation routine**: pull your official transcript from the reporting system, diff it against the tracker, enter **only what's missing**, and verify the official totals match yours before you renew.
- **A standing rhythm**: log each course when you finish it (or hand your agent the certificate and let it do the row), and a periodic check that reports hours banked vs. required, per category, per license — with the deficit, not just the total.

## Who this is for

Any licensed clinician — and it's built for the common hard case: **multiple licenses in multiple states**, each with its own cycle and categories, where one course may count toward several licenses (if each board accepts it) and a single missing ethics hour can hold up a renewal.

## How to use this kit

Hand the **whole folder** to your AI agent and say:

> "Set up my CE records using this kit. Start with the requirements worksheets in SKILL.md — one per license — before you touch my tracker."

Routes: native skill support (install the folder) / coding-workspace agent (point it at `SKILL.md`) / chat-only agent (upload all `.md` files, paste the prompt from `PROMPT.md`).

## Boundaries

- Your agent **prepares and enters records**; it never signs an attestation, checks a "I certify" box, or submits a renewal in your name without your explicit approval at that moment. Renewal is a legal act; it stays yours.
- Requirements change. Every worksheet value is re-verified against the board's current published rules at setup and at each renewal cycle — a number remembered from last cycle is a hypothesis, not a fact.
